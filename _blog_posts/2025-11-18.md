I little while back, I disabled automatic comment summarization because why bother
if people aren't interested in reading them. This is a free site after all and comment
summarization is not free.

However, they were not working at all for whatever reason. Thanks to Claude Code, the issue
is fixed and now they can be generated again if anyone is interested. Simply click the
"Summarize Comments" button.

I discovered that they were not working while poking around at the server. Next time, if
you see anything not working, or you have any suggestions for improvements on the site,
please consider [contacting me](https://x.com/emrahdma).

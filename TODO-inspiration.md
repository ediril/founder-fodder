✅ Top-Tier: Highest SEO & Story Leverage
These are the names that can realistically bring traffic, have proven search volume, and give you strong founder-centric story arcs.
Steve Jobs — massive demand; you can still win on long-tail angles; mandatory pillar content
Mark Cuban — huge awareness, startup-focused audience loves him
J. K. Rowling — rags-to-riches narrative; enormous global reach
Ralph Lauren — iconic brand builder with a classic bootstrap story
George Lucas — widely recognizable; independent creator → empire builder
John Paul DeJoria — textbook “from homeless to billionaire” story; founders eat this up
Debbi Fields — household brand + her story resonates hard with makers
Conrad Hilton — legacy entrepreneur; historical but high search relevance
Jessica Alba — celebrity crossover → startup founder; still strong traffic
Kendra Scott — skyrocketed in popularity, strong entrepreneurial arc

🔥 Top-Tier Additions (High Search Volume + Great Stories + Founder Appeal)
1. Andrew Carnegie
One of the most definitive rags-to-riches entrepreneurs in U.S. history.
Massive evergreen interest, essentially the blueprint of immigrant → industrialist.
2. Henry Ford
Iconic creator-founder.
Huge SEO, timeless story, and angles around perseverance + invention still work.
3. Jeff Bezos
Yes, he’s over-covered, but the long-tail search is monstrous,
and there are dozens of fresh angles you can use (garage myth-busting, early failures, slow years).
4. Sam Walton
Almost nobody writes deep pieces on his hardship and early failures.
Yet the search volume is evergreen and founder-relevant.
5. Phil Knight
His memoir ("Shoe Dog") gave his origin story cultural momentum.
Huge appeal for entrepreneurs and makers.
6. Estée Lauder
Perfect founder story: no money, no connections, self-made brand.
Her keyword space is surprisingly beatable with strong storytelling.
7. Walt Disney
Went bankrupt young, failed multiple times, and built a kingdom.
Still one of the single most searched entrepreneurs on earth.
8. Steve Wozniak
People massively underestimate how much search volume he pulls —
and he fits the “creator → builder” archetype better than Jobs for some readers.
9. Soichiro Honda
Insanely compelling founder arc: rejected by Toyota → founded Honda.
Large global search interest and very founder-oriented.
10. Ingvar Kamprad (IKEA)
Quietly one of the most searched non-U.S. billionaires.
Brutal childhood → frugal empire builder.
11. Ray Kroc
Late-bloomer story, heavy SEO demand, and plenty of conflict-driven narrative hooks.
Founders love these stories because they’re “never too late” case studies.
12. Story Musgrave or Ken Langone
Especially Langone (Home Depot) — strong adversity-based story, underrated traffic potential.
